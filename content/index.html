Offline music player
for your library.

Chilen streams music directly from your device. No account, no tracking - just you and your tunes!

FEATURES

What Chilen does

Chilen plays music from audio files on your device.

{% include shapes/cookie7.svg %}

cloud_off

Offline

Audio files are played directly off of your device. No connections to external services are made.

{% include shapes/clover4.svg %}

star

Beautiful

Following the Material Design 3 guidelines, Chilen has a beautiful user interface that feels responsive and is exceptionally easy to use.

{% include shapes/sunny.svg %}

rocket_launch

Fast

Built with Rust and Iced for maximum performance. Virtually no CPU usage, 200 MiB RAM utilization on idle with 1000 tracks loaded.

{% include shapes/cookie12.svg %}

verified_user

Private

No data ever leaves you device. Playlists, settings, listening statistics are never shared, until you decide to share them yourself.
